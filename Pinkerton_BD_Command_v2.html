📋 EPIC BD Cadence — Weekly Outreach Strategy

Week 1
First Touch
Send personalized intro emails to 15–20 Diamond/Gold Tier 1–2 prospects. LinkedIn connect the day before. Use the Intel Hook from the card as your specific opener. One ask: 15-min intro call. Log stage → CONTACTED.
Week 2
Follow-Up + Call
Follow up with value-add email (resource, not pitch). Attempt phone calls on top 5–10. Try a different contact at same organization if no reply. Move replies to ENGAGED. Log all touch attempts in notes.
Week 3
Angle Shift
New subject line, new EPIC service angle, or new decision-maker contact (Legal, HR, COO). Start fresh Week 1 batch of 15–20 new prospects. Flag Week 3 non-responders for 90-day re-engage.
Week 4
Final Ask + New Batch
Breakup email to non-responders. Move qualified leads to MEETING stage. Start new 15–20 prospect batch. Review reply rates — which EPIC angle, sector, and trigger is generating responses.

✅ Qualify / ❌ Disqualify Criteria

✅ Qualify — Continue Pursuing

  • 500+ employees with visible security function or budget signal
  • Documented public trigger (incident, litigation, expansion, regulatory)
  • 2+ EPIC service lines applicable (Silver or above)
  • Identifiable decision-maker with reachable contact
  • Responds to email or phone — even a "not now"
  • Expressed any pain point, even vaguely
  • Healthcare, transportation, finance, retail, manufacturing, education sector
  • No existing Pinkerton relationship in your territory

❌ Disqualify — Deprioritize

  • Under 100 employees, no visible security function
  • Municipal/government — long cycle, low margin
  • Already under active competitor contract
  • No identifiable contact after 2 research attempts
  • Single-service need, low budget signal
  • Hard no after 4 touches — move to 12-month re-engage
  • No footprint in OR, ID, UT, or MT
  • Confirmed fully in-house with zero consulting budget

💎 Multi-EPIC Scoring System

💎 Diamond — All 4 EPIC Lines
Highest Account Value
Needs span E+P+I+C. A single relationship could generate multi-year, multi-line revenue. Prioritize personal outreach, in-person introductions, and a full account plan from day one.
🥇 Gold — 3 EPIC Lines
Strong Multi-Line Opportunity
Lead with their single most urgent pain. Win the first engagement, then expand in the second meeting. Strong candidates for a proposed in-person intro meeting rather than just a call.
🥈 Silver — 2 EPIC Lines
Solid Single-Engagement + Expand
Win on the primary need, introduce the second EPIC line at the first QBR. Include in weekly email batches — don't skip, but don't over-invest in research time.
🥉 Bronze — 1 EPIC Line
Single-Line Engagement
One specific service fits. Worth a templated email if they're large and have a clear trigger. Don't over-invest outreach time — keep to 2-touch max before parking.
